##### Chapter 35

### Public-Utility Depreciation Policies

**Omission of Depreciation Charges.** In no field does the question of proper depreciation policy have such practical importance as in the public-utility group. Yet nowhere have there been wider variations in both theory and practice. Some years ago there were instances—notably that of Cities Service Company—of complete failure to make any deduction for depreciation (and depletion) in the annual reports, with a resultant gross misstatement of the earnings for the stock. The argument has often been advanced that depreciation charges may properly be ignored because they are mere bookkeeping entries and do not represent a real outlay of cash.
